**Answer: 0 seatings.**

With 5 seats around a round table, no seat is directly opposite another (that requires an even number of seats), so "C directly opposite E" can never be satisfied. There are no valid arrangements to list.

*(If "opposite" is loosened to mean "as far apart as possible" — two seats apart — then fixing A and placing D clockwise of A, B must sit two seats from A on the other side, giving exactly 2 seatings, read clockwise: A‑D‑C‑B‑E and A‑D‑E‑B‑C.)*
