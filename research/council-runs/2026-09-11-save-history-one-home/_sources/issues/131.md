# #131 A dedicated QA handheld with its own cloud accounts, so no test ever touches the maintainer's device or data [OPEN]

**Maintainer, 2026-09-11:** "We're dealing with production-level data here, and you changed it without asking, so that's a serious thing we need to avoid going forward. The intent was good. We just need to make sure the intent is happening the right way. For example, if we need a fully tested device, I'd rather invest in that, so that you always have a device to test against and separate accounts, etc., but that's a future discussion."

Parked as an open decision (D-QA-016). Until it is settled, the rule is D-QA-015: nothing runs on the maintainer's device or in their cloud without a per-action yes, and the VM with the QA endpoint answers everything it can (D-QA-007).

## What it would take

- A second H700 (or the RG SP, if it can be dedicated) on the LAN with ssh, flashed from the fork's images, holding no personal saves or ROMs -- a fixture tree like `seed-device`'s instead.
- Cloud accounts that hold nothing of the maintainer's: a Dropbox (and, when #113/#107 want it, a second provider) created for QA, signed in on that device only. The provider-specific behaviours the VM cannot reach -- Dropbox's hash, its lock contention, its transients, OAuth token renewal -- become testable without touching anyone's data.
- The runner (`tools/vm-qa`) gaining a device target beside the pair, with the same fixtures seeded and the same reports, so a device pass is a command rather than a session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A decision row records the device and the accounts chosen (or that the plan is declined).
- [ ] The exit test, the Dropbox replacement proof (#107) and the retry measurement (#113) run against the QA device and its accounts from the runner, with no step that reads or writes the maintainer's cloud.
