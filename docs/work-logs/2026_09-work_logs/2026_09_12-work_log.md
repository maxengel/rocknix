## 00:20 UTC -- one console at a time

The maintainer, after the council: sync never happens between two consoles
at once; the use case is a hand-off from the commute handheld to the home
one; concurrent use of one save folder is to be prevented, not reconciled
(D-CLOUD-102). A good part of the council's deliberation had gone to the
race between two publishers -- it was the argument that carried store-first
to its 3-2 win. With the race out of scope, the delta's original shape comes
back as the cheaper sufficient form: retain the displaced version from the
device's own stage (it restored it, so it has the bytes), then publish; two
uploads, no cloud-to-cloud copy, no escrow of every version, no `in_store`
flag. The council's engineering (transaction shape, deferral as a pair, the
protections, the audit, the migration) carries over untouched. Prevention
falls out of the manifests every pass already reads: a device behind another
device's publication restores before it publishes, and defers when it
cannot. Written up as `docs/save-history-serial-model-addendum.md` with the
choice: amend by hand, or a short council follow-up on the one question.
