## 00:50 UTC — #251 measured: stroke width against the grid, and the hinting lever that did nothing

The maintainer: *"is the issue strictly the font size, or is the font size rendering cleanly related to the resolution of the screen and/or its DPI?"* and *"will different fonts render more sharply because they're mathematically more viable on certain screen resolutions ... Are we just saying we'll make it bigger and hope it makes it clear?"* The code first: the KMS context reports no DPI (`drm_ctx.c`, `get_metrics` NULL), so RetroArch scales widgets from the pixel diagonal against 1080p (0.363 at 640x480), times the config's widget factor (1.0), times 1.5 for ozone; FreeType gets the size as an integer (`FT_Set_Pixel_Sizes`), rasterises with the font's own hinting under the modern interpreter (vertical snapping only), and the glcore driver draws each glyph at an integer origin, so nothing is resampled. Then the experiment (`exp-251.sh`, guest d, config and environment only): the toast at factor 1.0 (10 px) and 1.4 (15 px), each with and without `FREETYPE_PROPERTIES=truetype:interpreter-version=35` on RetroArch's process. Findings, measured on the x-height row (`solidity.py`): 10 px -- 41 strokes, none with a fully-lit core, mean stem 0.76 px; 15 px -- 46 strokes, 10 solid, 1.40 px; the achievement banner at 17 px -- 56 strokes, 21 solid, 1.37 px (the one the maintainer calls sharp); at 24 px -- 51 of 56 solid, 1.87 px, and the banner runs off the 640 px panel's right edge, which rules out the wider widget scale (option 3). The hinting variants were pixel-identical to the defaults: the lever does nothing without a font or a load-flag change. So: strictly size, chosen from resolution because DPI is unknown; sharpness is stem width against the grid -- Inter UI's stems cross one pixel near 13 px and two near 25 px here -- and "bigger" works for that reason, not by hope. Frames `docs/qa-frames/2026-09-24/251-*`. The maintainer's follow-on -- pick sizes per panel that land on the grid -- filed as its own issue.
