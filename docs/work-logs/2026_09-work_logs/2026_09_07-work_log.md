## 17:10 UTC — Gate 11: the spike scored, and the verdict (#9)

Task 8 to 10 in one sitting, on the pair. First the runner learned to
show its work: `--dump` writes every transport run's raw output to the
host, `moved:` names what rclone copied or renamed, the first conflict
line's bytes are printed before any parser trusted them, and item 9 counts
every rclone the pass spawns rather than the transfer alone. The dump paid
at once: item 5's "3 transferred" under bisync on WebDAV was bisync
downloading the both-changed pair to compare it — and skipping it as
equal — so the item is a PASS, and the runner's count had been reading a
hash download as a copy.

Then the variants: `--compare size,modtime,checksum --download-hash` makes

D-CLOUD-039's rule, so the verdict is the reconciler's own transport
(D-CLOUD-052) and D-CLOUD-044 is moot. Recorded on #9 with the grid and
the dumps, mirrored on #22, #9 closed, the epic's child ticked.
