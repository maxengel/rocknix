  reached nothing. vm-qa makes the folder before it seeds, which is why its
  own time-to-play suite passed throughout.

  fails; the probe ROM's copy and the restore's two copies are checked too.
  The retries stay, with their comment corrected. The comparison is re-run
  once run 100's chain frees guest d.
